# MWM paintbrush / friend-code bundles (viperfan91, diyc post-344802)

Source: DIYChristmas "Glow With Our Show" discussion thread, post-344802
(the "_Fiss_ blue glow v1" era), pasted ~2026-09. Decoded against
docs/mwm-show-protocol.md; the A-B-A' layering pattern is the same one
olp698 and the friend-code tool use.

Structure of each valid bundle:
  * <head>  = `96 19 xx .. 30 jj` broadcast paintbrush op (the "press"
    one hat sends so every friend hat replays the same effect locally).
  * <comp>  = `9x 24 58 vv ..` (or `9x 24 0D ..` / `9x 24 67 ..`) effect
    companion. Its FIRST byte after the header consolidates the head's
    trailing modifier; its LAST bytes are the effect instruction the hats
    actually run (dim/bright, pulse, fade, sway, per-ear cycle, ...).
  * Rows' trailing bytes are as-transcribed and, per the protocol doc's
    caveat, may not validate as CRC-8; treat payload fields as ground truth.

The flashing-ear rows (7, 8) label the ear that lights as "left" -- the
same physical-LEFT convention (the `0x08`/`0x80` override slot) the
library/tools/docs use since 2026-09.

## Valid bundles

1. both yellow, dim/bright alternating
   head: 96 19 0B 36 06 D4 30 01 7F
   comp: 99 24 58 03 48 0D D0 3E 32 66 6E BE
   (dim/bright modifier `D0 3E 32`, yellow)

2. both green, dim/bright
   head: 96 19 0B 12 10 97 BE 01
   comp: 99 24 58 08 48 0D D0 3E 32 62 6A 0B
   (dim/bright modifier `D0 3E 32`, green)

3. both white, fade up-hold ~2 s-down; companion 0x91
   head: 96 19 0D 3F 20 9E 30 11 81
   comp: 9B 24 67 6F D0 32 DF 5A 0A 08 10 48 91 4B
   (fade-up hold-down `D0 32 DF 5A 0A 08`; invoke effect 0x91 on hold)

4. 1 blue 1 yellow alternating
   head: 96 19 0E 31 0A 1C 30 04 9E
   comp: 9C 26 0C 1D 61 6E 58 EE 48 03 D1 3C 07 05 C2
   (alternating companion `58 EE`; blue `61`, yellow `6E`)

5. 1 blue 1 white swaying
   head: 96 19 0F 39 0C BE 30 05 79
   comp: 9D 24 58 F0 48 04 58 06 48 84 D0 3D 02 61 6F E4
   (sway companion `58 F0` / `D0 3D 02`; blue `61`, white `6F`)

6. both green pulsating
   head: 96 19 10 12 0E 68 30 03 9C
   comp: 99 26 62 6A D1 42 07 20 D0 32 F0 71
   (pulsate `D1 42 07 20 D0 32 F0`; green `62`, `6A`)
   Note: hide-and-show technique -- head carries the color, comp starts the
   geometry.

7. light blue, left/right/off cycle
   head: 96 19 11 1B 10 D0 30 01 50
   comp: 98 24 0D 63 6B 48 10 D0 42 08 E1
   (per-ear cycle `48 10 D0 42 08`: the "left" ear flashes, then the
   "right", then off)

8. green, left/right/off cycle
   head: 96 19 11 12 04 7B 42 01 85
   comp: 98 24 0D 62 6A 48 10 D0 42 02 95
   (per-ear cycle `48 10 D0 42 02`, green pairs `62`/`6A`)

## Corrupt as transcribed (excluded from use)

9. "1 blue 1 white alternating" -- illegal length headers (`75`, `D6`,
   `DF`, `9E` never appear on valid frames) and internally inconsistent
   lengths; kept for provenance only.
   chunk1: 96 D9 2A BB D2 C9 25 FD
   chunk2: DF A7 5E 7A D6 4B E5 4A DB D9 BE DB AA FF
   chunk3: D6 D9 2A BB D2 ED B6 F6

10. "both red pulsating" -- same corruption (headers `75`, `9E`, `96 66`).
   chunk1: 75 B5 B9 6F 99 BA 92 9A
   chunk2: 9E B6 6E DD 5D DB 76 36 BE BA 52 D2 D3 F7 FF
   chunk3: 96 66 A9 9A 2A AD B2 D2
